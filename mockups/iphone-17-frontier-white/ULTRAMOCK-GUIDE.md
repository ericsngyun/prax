# UltraMock Setup Guide

## Recommended five-shot setup

1. Start with the **iPhone 2** five-shot template.
2. Select the **iPhone 17** device in **Black**.
3. Assign the five PNG screens in numeric order, one image per shot.
4. Use a matte black, soft concrete, or seamless optical-white scene. Avoid Liquid Metal for this version.
5. Keep screen reflections very low so the white UI remains clean and neutral.
6. Use `brand/prax-wordmark-white-original.png` as a separate logo layer only on dark compositions.
7. These are complete iPhone screenshots with the Dynamic Island and status bar already included. Disable any additional automatic status-bar overlay to avoid duplication.

## Suggested motion

| Shot | Screen | Motion | Duration |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Intro | slow push in, slight low-angle tilt | 2.2s |
| 2 | Home | gentle top-to-bottom scan | 2.0s |
| 3 | Services | short truck right | 1.8s |
| 4 | Work | slow vertical scan | 2.2s |
| 5 | Confirmed | settle into a centered hero | 2.2s |

Keep the full teaser near 10–11 seconds with restrained ease-in-out transitions.

## Effects

- Screen reflection: off to low
- Motion blur: low
- Glass border: hairline only
- Liquid Glass: off or minimal
- Depth blur: subtle; preserve sharp white edges
- Lighting: one broad cool key and a very soft opposite rim

## Recommended exports

- Owner review: portrait `1080 × 1920`, 30 fps
- Social teaser: portrait `1080 × 1920`, 60 fps
- Website hero: landscape 4K with the phone offset from center
