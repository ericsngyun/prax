# UltraMock Setup Guide

## Device and screen fit

1. Choose **iPhone 17** with a `1206 × 2622` display slot.
2. Use the black device finish for maximum contrast against the optic-white UI.
3. Assign the five PNGs in numeric order with **Fit / 100% / no crop**.
4. Disable UltraMock’s automatic status-bar, Dynamic Island, and home-indicator overlays; those are already included.
5. Disable automatic perspective correction on the uploaded screen layer if it alters the full-screen aspect ratio.

## Recommended visual direction

- Scene: matte black, graphite stone, cool concrete, or seamless optic white
- Lighting: one broad cool key with a very soft opposite rim
- Reflection: off to low
- Glass or metallic effects: minimal
- Depth blur: subtle enough to keep the white screen edge crisp
- Device shadow: soft, low-opacity, and physically grounded
- Logo layer: use the white exact wordmark on dark scenes and the black derived treatment on light scenes

## Five-shot sequence

| Shot | Screen | Framing | Motion | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Home | centered three-quarter hero | slow 3–4% push | 2.0s |
| 2 | Artists | straight-on, screen legible | gentle top-to-bottom scan | 2.2s |
| 3 | Jack / Services | slight right offset | short truck left | 2.2s |
| 4 | Booking | close straight-on crop | controlled vertical settle | 2.0s |
| 5 | Confirmed | centered final hero | slow pull back | 2.2s |

Keep the finished teaser around 10–11 seconds. Use restrained ease-in-out transitions; avoid rapid spins or reflective sweeps that compete with the staff imagery and price information.

## Export recommendations

- Owner review: `1080 × 1920`, 30 fps, high-bitrate H.264
- Instagram/TikTok teaser: `1080 × 1920`, 60 fps
- Website hero: landscape 4K, phone offset to preserve copy space
- Still presentation: export each scene at 4K or higher before resizing

Use the PNG screens for final renders. WebP files are intended for quick previews, and SVG files are the editable masters.
